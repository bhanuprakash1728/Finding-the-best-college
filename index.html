Admissions, made clearer

A simple admission roadmap

The exact process varies by institution and course. Treat this as a checklist, then follow the current official admission notice.

See college admission links
1

Choose a course and shortlist

Match subjects and interests to course eligibility. Compare a few colleges, not just one.

2

Check eligibility and entrance exams

Read the official prospectus for required subjects, marks, exam scores, and any category-specific rules.

3

Apply before the deadline

Use the official application or counselling portal. Keep documents, a valid email, and payment receipts ready.

4

Follow counselling and confirm your seat

Track allotment rounds, verify the fee and refund rules, and complete document verification on time.

How to choose the right college

Look beyond rankings and make the choice that works for you.

📘

Course & curriculum

Check subjects, practical learning, faculty, accreditation, and whether the course supports your goals.

💼

Placement evidence

Read recent official reports. Look at roles, recruiters, median outcomes, and the number of students placed.

📍

Location & costs

Consider travel, accommodation, living expenses, campus safety, and access to support services.

🎓

Fit & support

Explore scholarships, student clubs, internships, accessibility, and the campus culture you want.
